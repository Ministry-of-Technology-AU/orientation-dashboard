# CAMPUS FACILITIES & SERVICES

## Courier Dispatch
The Administration department facilitates courier services.
Students may contact the reception desk on the ground floor of the admin building to send out their personal couriers on a payable basis between 5:00 pm – 5:30 pm on working days.
The consignees are requested to track their AWB numbers as the University is not accountable for the consignment at any stage.

## SIM cards
Mobile SIM cards may be obtained at the SIM Card kiosk (Location will be specified when the camp is held) after completing all necessary documentation during the days of the onboarding of the new batch in the new academic year.
SIM cards are available in prepaid and post-paid segments.
CUG and circle portability facility can be availed by the users.
This transaction is solely between the student and the service provider.

## Housekeeping in Residence Halls
Students are required to dispose of the trash into the central dustbin placed on the respective floors.
Cleaning of each washroom will be executed by the housekeeping staff on a regular basis as per predefined schedule pasted behind the washroom door.
Students’ rooms will be cleaned by housekeeping staff once a week, only in the presence of the student.
Weekly requests for cleaning have to be routed through the dedicated platform only (details will be updated soon).
Only emergency requirements may be reached through the housekeeping helpline +91-7496967703.

## Laundry Service
Centralised laundry service is available for students in the basement of Residence Hall 5 on a prefixed collection and return schedule, in standard laundry bags issued by the laundry vendor.
New students may collect laundry bags from the laundry located at Residence Hall 5 (ground floor) on a payment basis.
The approach to the basement of RH5 is through the side of the building (STP side), marked with a sign.
Residence hall washrooms are also equipped with washing machines for innerwear laundry.
Special dry cleaning of apparel may be availed on a payment basis from the laundry service provider.
A dedicated Laundry Room will be available at RH-6/7 for the students residing at RH-6&7 for the pick up & drop off of Laundry bags.

## ATM
ATM machines are installed on Campus, One on the rear side of the RH2 residence hall and another inside the HDFC bank lobby (Near Gate No.1).

## Mailroom
Mailroom facility for receiving any mail, including couriers, is in the basement of AC02, and this is accessible from the ramp leading from RH03.
The route is clearly marked out by signage.
The service is available from 9:30 AM to 1 PM and 2 PM to 5:30 PM on all working days.
The university shall not be deemed responsible if any courier is not collected.

## Lost and Found
The Lost and Found Desk is located at the reception on the ground floor of the Administration Block.
Any unattended item found may please be submitted at this desk.
The owner of the lost item may reclaim their possessions through our dedicated portal (details will be updated shortly) upon due authentication, if submitted to the desk by anyone.
Valuable items are retained for a period of 60 days, and non-valuable items for a period of 30 days.
Any lost or found items, after 5:30 PM & on the weekends, and holidays, are to be deposited at Main Gate No. 1.

## Spaces and Classrooms
All classes will remain open as per the timetable made by the timetable committees, and the lighting, air-conditioning are regulated according to the same timetable.
This is not applicable to the Multi-Purpose Hall (MPH) and Dr Reddy’s Auditorium, which will be opened based on the permission given by HOD/space booking portal.
In exceptional cases, spaces can be accessed upon prior intimation of a minimum of 12 hours with the approval of the HOD.

## Transport
Ashoka University facilitates commuting through a shuttle service available for students, faculty, staff and visitors to and from the Azadpur Metro Station, Ashoka University campus, and as per the approved and announced schedule.
However, shuttle services can be modified or suspended depending on the needs and requirements of stakeholders.
Users are advised to check their emails/app for updates regarding the same.
The primary objective of the shuttle is to provide safe, comfortable and efficient transportation for Students, staff and faculty.
The following guidelines and rules apply to the use of the shuttle service:
* Students may move in and out of campus in the designated campus shuttle service.
* Students must adhere to shuttle timings.
* Boarding and deboarding points are the campus gate nos 1 & 2, Azadpur Metro Station.
* Users should take care of their personal belongings.
* The University management will not be liable for any loss/damage of personal property on the shuttle.
* It is advised to reach the boarding point 10 minutes prior to the shuttle departure time.
* No request to delay the shuttle will be entertained.
* Always keep a copy of the shuttle schedule handy.
* Updated Shuttle Schedules are regularly sent via email and also visible on the app.
* Each shuttle vehicle will only carry passengers as per its seating capacity.
* Overcrowding is strictly prohibited.
* The shuttles will stop only at designated points.
* Midway pickup and drops / re-routing the shuttle are not allowed.
* Eatables are not allowed inside the shuttles to maintain cleanliness.
* Consumption of alcoholic products or tobacco, or contraband items, are strictly prohibited in the shuttles.
* Any changes in the rules & regulations will be communicated by the Transport Team in advance.
* All complaints should be escalated to the Transport Helpline number: 8222930514.

### Seat Booking in Shuttle Service
Users will be able to book a seat in the Shuttle in advance using the MoveInSync App.
All you have to do is log in to the app using your Ashoka email ID.
We have created a user guide manual which will guide you through the step-by-step process for linking your profile and booking a seat.
* **Shuttle Boarding Process**: Scan the QR code pasted inside the shuttle.
* **Booking Cancellation**: Booking cancellation is allowed up to the last minute before the shuttle departure time.
* If you book a seat and do not show up or do not cancel a booked seat, you will be treated as “No Show”.
* A person with three No Shows in a span of 30 days, may be blocked in the app for 15 days.
* They will have to take the shuttle by queuing at the boarding points, subject to availability of seats.
* If you encounter any difficulties in linking your profile or need any guidance related to the booking process, you can reach out to the transport team at transport@ashoka.edu.in.

## Medical Services & Guidelines
Ashoka University has its own 16-bedded Infirmary set up for primary healthcare.
The infirmary is located at the Sports Complex, Ground.
The following services are provided:
* First Aid and medical assistance, to the students, faculty and staff, around the clock.
* The infirmary is well equipped and manned by our medical partner, Blue Circle Medi Services Pvt. Ltd since the inception of Ashoka.
* The infirmary is equipped with adequate equipment like defib, ECG machine, Oxygen concentrators, and oxygen cylinders.
* Ashoka University has visiting doctor facilities as follows:
    * **Physiotherapist**: Six days a week (Monday to Saturday) from 2 pm – 8 pm
    * **Gynaecologist**: Once a week, Thursday, 3:00 pm – 5:00 pm
* The consultation is on first-cum-first served basis, thus you are requested to book your appointment at the Infirmary well in advance.
* Well-qualified and experienced Resident Doctors are available in the Infirmary round the clock.
* The cost of prescribed medicines and medical tests shall be borne by the patient.
* All students of the University are covered under an insurance of INR 1.5 Lacs applicable upon hospitalization of 24 hours or more.
* Ashoka has tie-ups with Max Hospital on a pan-India basis, Fortis Hospital, Delhi- NCR, and locally with Tulip and Park Nidaan Hospital.
* **24x7 Infirmary emergency helpline numbers**: 8199977073 | 8199977075 | 0130-2300550
* The Campus is equipped with two 24X7 ambulances.
* One Patient Transport Ambulance and one Basic Life Support Ambulance are available.
* Ambulance assistance would be provided only in case of any medical emergency and the need for hospitalisation.
* On such occasions, the matter is reported to the concerned authorities immediately by the nursing staff.
* The nurse would accompany the student only during admission and discharge, and on an exceptional case-by- case basis to the nearest hospital/nursing home.
* In case the ambulances are already out on duty, adequate backup is available, and cars will be provided for patient transfer to the external care provider, Max Hospital, etc.

## Campus Security
In case of emergency, an evacuation exit plan is prominently displayed at suitable locations on Campus.
Safe Assembly Areas for evacuation drills in case of fire or other disaster have been demarcated within the University Campus.
Students are required to become familiar with the exit plans.
* Quarterly mock drills are conducted to handle fire and safety emergency situations.
* The campus is equipped with an advanced firefighting system along with trained QRT members.
* The entire university is compliant to the governing fire and safety norms of GOI.
* Students will be responsible for the safe- keep of their personal belongings and valuables, such as cash, jewellery, laptop, camera, cell phone, etc.
* All cupboards in residence halls are provided with lockable drawers.
* However, in case of theft, students will report the matter to the respective warden.
* Policies and procedures in vogue for ensuring safety and security, on and off Campus, are to be adhered to by the students in letter and spirit.
* All related policies are available on the Ashoka portal.
* **Security helpline number (24x7)**: 8199977071 | 01302300151

## Maintenance & Engineering Services
Ashoka University Campus is well equipped with all infrastructural amenities to ensure a comfortable stay for students:
* **Air-Conditioning (Cooling)**: Room temperature will be maintained in the range of 26 ± 1 degrees Celsius with the fan operational.
* **Air-Conditioning (Heating)**: Room temperature will be maintained in the range of 18 ± 1 degrees Celsius.
* Air-conditioning services at the residence halls are available in accordance with the following schedule:
    * **Weekdays**: from 6:00 pm to 6:00 am
    * **Weekends and holidays**: 24 hours
* Electrical and power supply (including emergency power supply) is available 24x7, except during scheduled power outages for conducting preventive maintenance/up-gradation activities.
* In cases of scheduled power outage, prior communication will be made by the Maintenance team.
* **Hot Water Timings**:
    * **Weekdays**: Morning - From 07:00 HRS to 10:00 HRS and Evening 19:00 HRS to 22:00 HRS
    * **Weekend**: Morning - From 08:00 HRS to 11:00 HRS and Evening 20:00 HRS to 23:00 HRS
* **RO Purified Drinking Water**: Domestic water is available around the clock in the residence halls.
* However, on days scheduled for cleaning of water tanks, water will not be available for two hours.
* On such occasions, prior communication will be made by the Maintenance team.
* The Maintenance team on Campus can be contacted through the webpage for all maintenance-related requests/complaints.
* In case of any emergency, please call our 24/7 Maintenance helpline number mentioned below.
* The following operations also fall under the scope of maintenance and repair:
    * Lift operations
    * Interiors and civil works on the Campus
    * Fire-fighting and fire detection systems for safe operations in the buildings
