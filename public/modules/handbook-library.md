# LIBRARY SERVICES

## Welcome to HDFC Library @ Ashoka!
HDFC library will be your trusted partner in your academics and research pursuits. It strives to provide you with information resources required for your curriculum and beyond. HDFC library offers a very comfortable & conducive environment for study & reference. This large facility (30,000 sq ft) is fully carpeted & provided with sound absorbers to ensure quiet reading. Spread across two floors in the AC 04 building, it offers different seating options, colour zones & modular seats. It's fully automated with self-check-in / check-out machines, catalogue searching kiosks, QR-based registration systems and hybrid Electromagnetic gates.

When the academic sessions are in progress, the library is open 24x7, except for Friday nights (11 pm to 7 am the next morning). However, during summer (June & July), the timings may be slightly tweaked and will be intimated to all stakeholders.

Every UG student can borrow up to three books for a period of two weeks at a time. Books can be renewed/re-issued for two times, provided it is not reserved by others. During renewals, books are to be shown/presented in the library. Students may use the self-check in machines for borrowing, renewals and returns for easy & smooth transactions. Fines for late returns are charged at the rate of Rs. 10 per day per book.

Reference/Not for Loan books are not to be issued but to be consulted in the library premises. Bags(including laptop covers/Pouches) / eatables/beverages (except water) are not allowed inside the library. Please note there are water dispensers on both floors. While you may relax a bit or have a short power nap, you are not permitted to lie down / sleep in the library.

The library catalogue (OPAC) which is the gateway to search books, find your borrowings, track renewals, etc is available from the link <Koha.ashoka.edu.in>. Alternatively, you can access it through the Ashoka library portal or the touch screen kiosks in the library. We would encourage you to access the resources using the Library Mobile app.

Important services include books borrowing, inter library loans, reading, reference services, documents supply, plagiarism check, research support and remote access to a wide variety of online journals, books, e-databases & e-reference sources.

If any of the required documents (books, journal articles, theses, etc) are not available with the library, attempts will be made to get them from partner institutions and resource centres through Inter Library Loan of DELNET Services. Feel free to write to us at (library@ashoka.edu.in). Please note that this may take time as it depends on the availability of the books with other member institutions.

### Digital Research Support Service
The Digital Research Support Service (DRSS) is a dedicated platform for researchers, where they can explore research based on their educational needs and view other researchers' work to gain deeper knowledge. HDFC Library has launched a comprehensive new platform called the Digital Research Support Service (DRSS), designed to assist researchers and organisations in their quest for valuable knowledge and resources. This innovative initiative provides access to a vast repository of content across various subjects, aiming to foster collaboration and enhance research efforts. The DRSS portal offers a wide range of curated resources, including e-books, e-journals, and e-databases, all organised according to the specific needs of individual user profiles.

This platform not only supports individuals in their personal academic endeavours but also serves as a valuable tool for organisations looking to foster innovation and advance their research capabilities. Through this initiative, HDFC Library continues to provide meaningful resources that contribute to the growth and success of the global research community.

HDFC Library has three major components in its DRSS, which help researchers find their required information in a specific subject domain and help to discuss their thoughts, their obstacles and their support to other researchers. These components are-

* **Knowledge Guide:** A tailored tool designed to help you easily discover research materials that align with your specific interests and academic goals. Personalising the content ensures that you have access to the most relevant and relevant resources to enhance your research efforts.
* **Research Profile:** A dedicated platform where you can present and showcase your research interests, achievements, and academic progress. This feature allows you to track the evolution of your work, helping you stay organised and monitor key milestones in your academic journey.
* **Research Forum:** An interactive space that fosters meaningful connections with fellow researchers, experts, and thought leaders. It provides an opportunity to share ideas, collaborate on projects, and engage in insightful discussions, enabling a vibrant exchange of knowledge and expertise within the research community. It also helps in interdisciplinary research among researchers.
