# Information Technology Services - Student Handbook

The Department of Information Technology (IT) at Ashoka University is the primary information-technology provider on the campus. Our mission is to provide and promote IT services that support the vision and mission of the University and provide quality IT services to the university community and guests.

The IT Department offers support for:

- Internet Access and Device configuration during orientation on campus
- Managing the Ashoka email ID of all students and Alums
- Managing the official mailing lists and access rights for the same
- Using the various software applications hosted by the University
- Audio Visual Equipment on campus

---

## Ashoka Email ID

All students at Ashoka University are assigned an Ashoka email ID after they have enrolled in a program at Ashoka. The format of the email ID is:

`FirstName.LastName_<ProgramCode><yy>@ashoka.edu.in`

Once you receive the credentials on your registered email id:

- Navigate to mail.ashoka.edu.in or mail.google.com to sign into your Ashoka email.
- All communication after orientation will be done on this email ID
- Access the Ashoka Intranet (my.ashoka.edu.in) using your Ashoka email ID

After graduation:

- The student email ID will be deleted one month after graduation and will be registered as an Alumni with an alumni email ID.
- The responsibility of saving important/migrating emails and documents for the student email ID lies with the student
- We request you to not use your Ashoka email ID for registration at sites/ services that you may need to access after your graduation.

## IT Registration

IT Registration is a mandatory enrollment process for all incoming students. During the registration process in the orientation week, the IT Support Team will facilitate:

### a. Biometric Registration (for YIF students only)

Student fingerprints are registered as a requirement from the YIF program for the following purposes:

- Tracking student classroom attendance, if applicable.

The biometric tracking data is accessible to the YIF program team daily to ensure appropriate adherence to classroom attendance. Upon completion of the exit process from the University, the biometric data (fingerprints) of all the YIF students is erased permanently from our systems.

### b. Laptop/Device Configuration - Network User ID and password

During IT registration, student laptops will be configured to access the Ashoka Wi-Fi network and checked for an active antivirus subscription. A network User ID and password will be provided to each student to access the internet on the campus. This process will take 10 to 15 minutes and will be done in your presence.

Students are required to have an active subscription to an antivirus software on their laptops. This is necessary to enhance the security of our networks. The registration process will take longer if you do not have the antivirus installed.

A maximum of two devices can be registered to use the Ashoka network. IT Support will scan the devices in your presence for any unregistered/pirated/harmful application(s) that might affect the University's IT network.

The students will be charged a license fee for a genuine antivirus software if a laptop is found without a genuine software.

### c. Recommended configuration for laptops

Students are encouraged to bring laptops of the best configuration possible within their personal budgets and preferences. We recommend the following configuration in the interest of standardizing the IT environment to ensure a high level of efficiency in service and support for all students and faculty members:

- **Processor:** Intel® Core™ i5 processor or above
- **Operating System:** Win 8.1 or above (For Apple Machine: MAC OS 10 or above)
- **Hard Drive:** 500 GB and above
- **Memory:** 4GB or above
- **Communication:** Wireless (802.11a/g/n) compliant – 10/100/1000 Ethernet
- **Power adaptor:** compatible with Indian 3-pin 5-amp socket
- Preferably HDMI/VGA connector – For projection needs
- Preferably an in-built camera – For any soft video conference
- Minimum 1 USB Port

Please note that this configuration is not mandatory in any way, and we will try our best to support all the devices that you bring on to the campus.

## IT Services

### a. Network Connectivity Support

i. Wi-Fi services are available in all parts of the campus. Students are requested to abide by the guidelines for connecting to Wi-Fi as outlined in the internet policy. Failing to follow this process will lead to suspension of all connectivity.
ii. Computer laboratories are equipped with wired connectivity (LAN)

### b. Standby Laptop Support

i. The IT team is not responsible for any hardware related issues/any other kind of repairs/or maintenance of laptops/devices. However, it may assist in the resolution of any such issue by providing the contact details concerning the manufacturer/ service center.
ii. Standby laptops may be provided, subject to availability, for a maximum of one week after a student submits the Requisition Form, while their laptop is being repaired.
iii. Failure to return the laptop on time will lead to the suspension of all IT services and facilities until the laptop is returned.
iv. All accidental/intentional damages/ loss sustained on the standby laptop will be borne by the student.

### c. Smart Card Support

i. Smart card enabled student ID cards are configured by the IT Systems team and issued to all incoming students by the Office of Residence Life/Academic Program teams.
ii. Students are required to use these smart cards at dining kiosks, gate entry/exit and for daily hostel attendance.
iii. In case a smart card is lost/misplaced, students should immediately report the loss to the issuing authority (Office of Residence Life/Academic Program Team) and request a replacement after paying a replacement fee.

### d. Printing Support

i. Printing/scanning services can be availed at the documentation centre in the Student Commons room, which is located on 2nd Floor, Dining Block (documents.centre@ashoka.edu.in).
ii. These services are provided on a chargeable basis.

### e. Audio Visual Support

i. For any audio-visual support request other than the regular sessions, a student may contact the IT Helpdesk team with the details of the event/session and with the specific requirements.
ii. The team shall assess the requirements and recommend a venue depending on the services required. Additionally, the student will need to coordinate with the concerned department(s) to block the suggested venue.
